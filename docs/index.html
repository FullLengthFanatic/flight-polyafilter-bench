polyAfilter across chemistries and materials

Internal priming burden and site-level agreement on seven retina-organoid single-cell libraries.

Simone Picelli (ORCID). Updated 2026-11-03. Companion summary of the flight-polyafilter-bench repo (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23562408).

Summary

polyAfilter removes internal-priming artefacts from 3' single-cell RNA-seq by flagging alignments whose 20 nt downstream of the 3' end on the reference carry a run of genomic adenines (Svoboda, Frost, and Bosco 2022). The question is how much that burden varies across chemistries and materials, and whether the filter behaves consistently across the four combinations.

Seven retina-organoid libraries were used: two week-26 10x 3' v4 nuclei preps (1417, 1419), one week-26 10x 3' v4 cells prep (1179), one week-26 BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq cells prep on the same cell pool as 1179 (BD67), and three week-26 BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq nuclei preps on three donors (BD_nuc_211, 213, 215, with 211 sharing the donor of 1419). Templated-share burden was estimated per library with a logit-OLS correction and a gene-cluster bootstrap. A 2x2 interaction model fits chemistry (10x vs BD) crossed with material (cells vs nuclei) and reports the contrasts and the interaction coefficient. Site-level agreement is reported as Spearman correlation of per-site usage over shared sites, both at full depth and depth-matched by downsampling the deeper member of each pair.

Headline. The templated burden falls from 10x cells (49.7%) to BD cells (25.9%) and rises from both to the matched nuclei preps (89.2% on 10x, 40.2% on BD). The chemistry effect averaged over material is a 36 point absolute drop (BD below 10x); the material effect averaged over chemistry is a 27 point absolute rise (nuclei above cells); the interaction is a 25 point attenuation of the material effect on BD. Site-level agreement between materials, depth-matched, is 0.849 on 10x and 0.843 on BD; the within-material baseline is 0.885 on 10x (1417 vs 1419) and 0.892 on BD (BD_nuc_213 vs BD_nuc_215). The chemistry effect is large and consistent; the material effect is present on both chemistries but much smaller on BD, and the matched-site structure is preserved at comparable fidelity.

1. Rationale

Oligo-dT scRNA-seq primes on any run of adenines in the mRNA pool, including genomic A-stretches inside the body of a transcript. When it does, it generates a read that looks like a legitimate 3' end but represents nothing. polyAfilter detects these events by scanning the 20 nt downstream of each 3' end on the reference, counting adenines, and labelling sites templated (likely internal priming) or non-templated (likely a real polyA tail).

Two confounders decide how much this matters in a given library. Material is first: nuclei preps capture pre-mRNA, which carries intronic A-stretches that cells have mostly spliced out, so nuclei carry more internal-priming substrate. Chemistry is second: a bead-attached oligo-dT (BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq) and a droplet oligo-dT (10x Genomics) differ in capture geometry, enzyme chemistry, and bead-tail handling, and these are known to shift the balance between templated and real priming.

Separating the two matters because the filter's recall and the UMI cost it incurs depend on them. If a reader is picking a chemistry or a tool configuration, they need to know whether a filter calibrated on 10x cells will behave the same on BD nuclei. The 2x2 design, with per-cell replicates on the BD-nuclei arm, gives the contrast.

Both main effects are in the same direction across the other factor. BD cells (0.259) sit below 10x cells (0.497) by 24 points, and BD nuclei (0.402) sit below 10x nuclei (0.892) by 49 points. Nuclei sit above cells by 40 points on 10x and by 14 points on BD. The interaction coefficient is the gap between those two material effects: 25 points of attenuation on BD. The sign is consistent across every bootstrap draw. The CI width (0.085 points on the share scale) is driven by the three BD-nuclei libraries, which carry the bootstrap variability on that cell.

Interpretation. Chemistry is the dominant axis: BD Rhapsody has a markedly lower internal-priming burden than 10x at the same material. Material matters on both chemistries, but the gap between cells and nuclei is roughly a third as large on BD as it is on 10x. For a user picking a prep: BD-cells is the lowest-burden combination tested; 10x-nuclei is the highest; moving from 10x-cells to BD-cells cuts the burden roughly in half; moving from cells to nuclei on BD adds roughly 14 points, versus 40 on 10x.

4. Site-level agreement (Panel G)

Templated share is one summary. The matched-sites analysis asks a harder question: do the two sides of a pair recover the same 3' sites with the same relative usage. Sites are matched within a 5 nt tolerance; shared sites keep a usage count on each side; genes with at least 20 molecules on both sides are retained; Spearman correlation is computed on the per-site usage across the shared gene set. For cross-material pairs the deeper member is downsampled 100 times to the shallower member's molecule count, and the depth-matched rho is the mean across draws with a 95% range.

Within-material baselines are reported alongside the cross-material pair to anchor what "good" looks like.

Interpretation. The within-material baselines are nearly identical across chemistries (0.885 on 10x, 0.892 on BD), so the two systems resolve 3' sites with comparable per-donor reproducibility. The cross-material correlation on 10x drops to 0.661 at full depth because 1179 is 11 to 13× deeper than each of the two nuclei libraries at the library level; once downsampled to match, it recovers to 0.849. On BD the full-depth and depth-matched values are essentially equal (0.841 and 0.843), because the pooled flight_nuclei side at shared sites is only about 9× deeper than BD67. The matched-site structure is preserved by polyAfilter at comparable fidelity across the two chemistries.

5. Practical guidance

  • Internal priming is the dominant 3' artefact on oligo-dT scRNA-seq nuclei. On 10x nuclei it accounts for about 86% of molecules after correction. Any downstream analysis that treats a 3' end as evidence of a cleavage-polyadenylation site needs filtering.
  • Chemistry sets the baseline burden. At the same material, BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq runs 24 to 49 points below 10x in corrected templated share. A filter calibrated on 10x will be more aggressive than needed on BD; a filter calibrated on BD will under-correct on 10x.
  • Material matters more on 10x than on BD. Moving from cells to nuclei adds 40 points on 10x and 14 on BD. If a reader is choosing between cells and nuclei for a mixed workflow, the UMI cost of filtering will be more sensitive to that choice on 10x.
  • Matched-site structure is preserved across chemistries. Depth-matched Spearman ρ between nuclei and cells is 0.849 on 10x and 0.843 on BD, both close to the within-material baselines. polyAfilter does not reshape the 3' site landscape in a chemistry-specific way at the depth these libraries carry.
  • Per-library settings are tabulated in docs/parameter_choices.md, with sens/spec/UMI-cost/ARI for the two chosen cut-offs on each prep.
