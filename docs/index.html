polyAfilter across chemistries and materials

Internal priming burden and site-level agreement on seven human retina single-cell libraries.

Simone Picelli (ORCID). Updated 2026-11-11. Companion summary of the flight-polyafilter-bench repo (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23562408).

Summary

In 3' single-cell RNA-seq the oligo(dT) primer also binds A-rich stretches inside transcripts. This is internal priming, and polyAfilter (Svoboda, Frost, and Bosco 2022) is a filter that removes reads lying just upstream of such stretches in the genome. This page asks how much internal priming each library carries, and how that burden varies across two chemistries and two materials. How well the filter removes it is covered in docs/parameter_choices.md.

Seven human retina libraries were used: two 10x 3' v4 nuclei preps from adult donor retina (1417, 1419), one 10x 3' v4 cells prep from week-26 retinal organoids (1179), one BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq cells prep on the same cell pool as 1179 (BD67), and three BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq nuclei preps from adult donor retina (BD_nuc_211, 213, 215, with 211 sharing the donor of 1419). The burden is the share of uniquely mapped labeled molecules that are templated, meaning their priming site is followed by 8 or more A's in the 20 nt of genome downstream. It is given raw and corrected for label purity, with a gene bootstrap interval. A 2x2 interaction model fits chemistry (10x vs BD) crossed with material (cells vs nuclei) and reports the contrasts and the interaction coefficient. Site-level agreement is reported as Spearman correlation of per-site usage over shared sites, both at full depth and depth-matched by downsampling the deeper member of each pair.

Headline. The raw templated burden falls from 10x cells (48.4%) to BD cells (19.4%) and rises from each to the nuclei of the same chemistry (89.1% on 10x, 37.0% on BD). The chemistry effect averaged over material is a 41 point absolute drop (BD below 10x); the material effect averaged over chemistry is a 29 point absolute rise (nuclei above cells); the interaction is a 23 point attenuation of the material effect on BD. Site-level agreement between materials, depth-matched per-gene to the minimum of the two sides, is 0.854 on 10x and 0.883 on BD; the within-material baseline is 0.888 on 10x (1417 vs 1419) and 0.889 on BD (BD_nuc_213 vs BD_nuc_215). The chemistry effect is large and consistent; the material effect is present on both chemistries but less than half as large on BD, and site usage agrees between nuclei and cells almost as well as between two nuclei libraries once depth is matched. All nuclei libraries are adult donor retina and both cell libraries are organoids, so the material effect cannot be separated from tissue.

1. Rationale

Oligo-dT scRNA-seq primes on any run of adenines in the mRNA pool, including genomic A-stretches inside the body of a transcript. When it does, it generates a read that looks like a legitimate 3' end but represents nothing. The benchmark finds these events from reads that reach the poly(A): the end of their alignment is the priming site. It counts adenines in the 20 nt of reference downstream of the site and labels the molecule templated (8 or more, likely internal priming), non-templated (4 or fewer, likely a real poly(A) tail) or ambiguous (5 to 7).

Two confounders decide how much this matters in a given library. Material is first: nuclei preps capture pre-mRNA, which carries intronic A-stretches that cells have mostly spliced out, so nuclei carry more internal-priming substrate. Chemistry is second: FLIGHT-seq runs reverse transcription on the BD Rhapsody bead with a FLASH-seq type chemistry, 10x releases its primer into the droplet, and either difference could shift the balance between templated and real priming.

Separating the two matters to a reader who is picking a chemistry or a material. The 2x2 design, with three libraries on the BD-nuclei arm, gives the contrast. It has one limit to keep in view: the nuclei are adult donor retina and the cells are organoids, so material is confounded with tissue.

With them included the raw share would be 0.259 for BD67 and 0.38 to 0.44 for the BD nuclei; the 10x rows change by 1.3 points at most.

3. Chemistry by material interaction (Panel F)

The 2x2 model is a logit-OLS fit of the raw templated share (share_label in Table 1, not the corrected column) on chemistry (10x, BD) crossed with material (cells, nuclei), with each library a replicate within its cell. Standard errors come from 1000 gene-cluster bootstrap draws per library with an independent random stream per library (derived from a parent seed via numpy.random.SeedSequence.spawn). Fitted cell means are back-transformed to the share scale for display; contrasts are reported on both the share scale and the logit scale.

These intervals describe gene-resampling uncertainty conditional on the observed libraries. They do not estimate biological-replicate variability across donors or batches; with one whole-cell library per chemistry, the cell arms carry no between-donor spread. The material contrast is also a tissue contrast (adult retina nuclei, organoid cells). Read the interaction as descriptive, not as an isolated causal chemistry effect.

Both main effects are in the same direction across the other factor. BD cells (0.194) sit below 10x cells (0.484) by 29 points, and BD nuclei (0.370) sit below 10x nuclei (0.891) by 52 points. Nuclei sit above cells by 41 points on 10x and by 18 points on BD. The interaction coefficient is the gap between those two material effects: 23 points of attenuation on BD. The sign is consistent across every bootstrap draw. The CI width (0.082 on the share scale) is driven by the three BD-nuclei libraries, which carry the bootstrap variability on that cell.

Interpretation. Chemistry is the dominant axis: BD Rhapsody has a markedly lower internal-priming burden than 10x at the same material. Material matters on both chemistries, but the gap between cells and nuclei is less than half as large on BD as it is on 10x. For a user picking a prep: BD-cells is the lowest-burden combination tested; 10x-nuclei is the highest; moving from 10x-cells to BD-cells cuts the burden by more than half; moving from cells to nuclei on BD adds roughly 18 points, versus 41 on 10x.

4. Site-level agreement (Panel G)

Templated share is one summary. The matched-sites analysis asks a harder question: do the two sides of a pair show the same priming sites with the same relative usage. No filter is applied here; the sites are those of the labeled molecules. Sites are matched within a 5 nt tolerance; shared sites keep a usage count on each side; genes with at least 20 molecules on both sides are retained; Spearman correlation is computed on the per-site usage across the shared gene set. For cross-material pairs both sides are drawn to min(count_a, count_b) molecules per gene over 100 draws (per-gene minimum downsampling); the depth-matched rho is the mean across draws with a 95% range.

Within-material baselines are reported alongside the cross-material pair to anchor what "good" looks like.

Interpretation. The within-material baselines are nearly identical across chemistries (0.888 on 10x, 0.889 on BD), so the two systems resolve 3' sites with comparable per-donor reproducibility. Under per-gene minimum downsampling, the 10x cross-material correlation rises from 0.662 at full depth to 0.854 (close to the within-material 10x baseline), and the BD cross-material correlation rises from 0.840 at full depth to 0.883, which exceeds the 10x depth-matched value and sits just under the FLIGHT within-material baseline. BD has the larger depth-matched cross-material rho. A likely reason, not tested here, is that the depth asymmetry runs the opposite direction (flight_nuclei deeper than BD67, 1179 deeper than 1417+1419), which leaves the 10x shared sites with fewer molecules at the depth-matched point. Site usage is shared between nuclei and cells at comparable fidelity on the two chemistries.

5. Practical guidance

  • Internal priming is the dominant 3' artefact on oligo-dT scRNA-seq nuclei. On 10x nuclei it accounts for about 86% of labeled molecules after correction. Any downstream analysis that treats a 3' end as evidence of a cleavage-polyadenylation site needs filtering.
  • Chemistry sets the baseline burden. At the same material, BD Rhapsody FLIGHT-seq runs 29 to 52 points below 10x in raw templated share (27 to 57 after correction). The settings selected on 10x nuclei keep their specificity on FLIGHT-seq (0.97 to 0.99).
  • Material matters more on 10x than on BD. Moving from cells to nuclei adds 41 points on 10x and 18 on BD. The nuclei are adult retina and the cells organoids, so part of each gap may be tissue.
  • Priming sites are shared between nuclei and cells. Depth-matched Spearman ρ of site usage between nuclei and cells is 0.854 on 10x and 0.883 on BD, close to the within-material baselines (0.888 and 0.889).
  • Per-library settings are tabulated in docs/parameter_choices.md, with sensitivity, specificity, UMI cost and ARI for the selected settings on each prep.
