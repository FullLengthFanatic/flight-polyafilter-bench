# Methods text for the FLIGHT-seq manuscript

Drafts to adapt for the manuscript. The reasoning behind each choice is in `docs/rationale.md`; the scope limits listed there apply. The DOI below is the Zenodo concept DOI, which resolves to the latest release; at submission, replace it with the DOI of the release that holds the numbers cited.

## Short Methods (one paragraph, main text)

Internal priming was quantified, and its removal by polyAfilter (Svoboda, Frost and Bosco 2022) evaluated, with a reproducible benchmark (Picelli 2026, [10.5281/zenodo.23562408](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23562408)). Reads whose 3' end reached the poly(A) mark the priming site of their molecule. Each such molecule was labelled from the 20 nt of genome downstream of the site: templated (internal priming) with 8 or more adenines, non-templated (tail priming) with 4 or fewer, ambiguous with 5 to 7. The cut-offs were set on one library from the rate of the poly(A) signal (AATAAA or ATTAAA, 5 to 35 nt upstream) at each adenine count. polyAfilter was scored on the remaining reads of the labelled molecules, at 396 parameter settings, with a reimplementation that removes the same records as the original tool in all 27 runs compared. One setting per mode was selected on a nuclei library as the most sensitive at a specificity of at least 0.95 (`c250_l7_m1_e` without introns, `c200_l10_m2_i` with introns) and reported on the other libraries without retuning, next to the example setting of the polyAfilter documentation (`c300_l10_m1_e`). Long-read 3' ends were classified with tecap (Picelli 2026, [github.com/FullLengthFanatic/tecap](https://github.com/FullLengthFanatic/tecap)).

## Long Methods (supplementary methods)

**Labels.** In Cell Ranger BAMs, reads whose poly(A) was clipped carry the `pa` tag; for BD Rhapsody BAMs the tag was derived from the soft-clipped poly(A) (20 nt or more). The alignment 3' end of such a read is the priming site of its molecule (cell barcode, UMI, strand, gene). Molecules were labelled by the number of adenines in the 20 nt of reference downstream of the site: templated at 8 or more, non-templated at 4 or fewer, ambiguous at 5 to 7. The cut-offs were fixed on library 1417 before any filter result was examined, at the adenine counts where the rate of a poly(A) signal hexamer (AATAAA or ATTAAA, 5 to 35 nt upstream of the site) had covered a quarter of the distance between its level at 0 to 4 adenines (0.77) and at 18 to 20 adenines (0.02). The same cut-offs were used in every library. Because real poly(A) sites followed by genome-encoded adenines are also labelled templated, a corrected share was computed per library from the hexamer rate of each adenine band relative to the rate at 14 or more adenines. All figures refer to molecules from called cells.

**Accuracy.** polyAfilter (commit 5ce0098) was evaluated on the other reads of each labelled molecule within 1,000 nt upstream of the site, which the filter judges without access to the site. A molecule counted as removed when all its reads were removed. Sensitivity is the share of templated molecules removed, specificity the share of non-templated molecules kept; intervals are from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of genes. The grid covered COVLEN 150 to 600, MINSNRLEN 3 to 20, MISM 0 to 2, with and without introns, plus the published settings (396 settings). All settings were flagged in one pass by a reimplementation that reuses polyAfilter's A-stretch scanner; its removed records were identical to those of the original tool at ten settings on 1417, eight on 1419, eight on chr21 and chr22 of 1179 and one remove-all control. The selection rule was fixed in advance: on 1417, per mode, the setting with the highest sensitivity among those with specificity of at least 0.95. Library 1419 (second donor) and the whole-cell libraries served as hold-outs.

**Impact.** At 14 settings, UMIs were recounted with Cell Ranger's documented rule (per cell and UMI, the gene with most reads; ties discarded). UMI cost is the sum of UMIs lost over the sum of UMIs before filtering, across called cells. Cells were clustered with scanpy (2,000 variable genes, 30 principal components, 15 neighbours, Leiden with the igraph flavour) at 10 seeds and 3 resolutions; the adjusted Rand index (ARI) against the unfiltered clustering is reported as the median, together with the ARI of the unfiltered matrix clustered with other seeds (ceiling) and of the unfiltered matrix thinned at random to the filtered UMI totals.

**Long reads.** Priming sites were read from two PacBio Kinnex libraries (BD67, same organoid cell pool as 1179; 10x_FL, a week-29 organoid 10x library) as the start of the terminal adenine run (10 nt or more, one non-adenine allowed; for BD67 followed by at least 30 nt of bead sequence). A long-read site within 10 nt was found for 87% (BD67) and 95% (10x_FL) of the labelled 1179 molecules, against 6% and 17% after moving the sites 1 kb upstream. Labels at matched sites agreed in 99.4% and 99.5% of cases, as expected for labels read from the same reference sequence.

**Burden across chemistries and materials.** The templated share was computed on molecules whose poly(A) reads all map uniquely (STAR MAPQ 255); sensitivity and specificity use all labelled molecules. The share of the seven libraries was compared with an ordinary least squares fit of the logit share on chemistry (10x, FLIGHT-seq), material (cells, nuclei) and their interaction, with intervals from 1,000 gene bootstrap draws per library. The intervals describe gene resampling given the observed libraries, not variability between donors. Nuclei were adult donor retina and cells were retinal organoids, so the material contrast is descriptive.
